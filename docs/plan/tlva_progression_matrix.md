# TLVA Learning TV – Progression Matrix (K–6)

## Purpose
Define how the same content templates scale from kindergarten through grade 6, so creation remains reusable and delivery is adaptive.

## Grade Bands
- Band A: K–1 (ages ~5–7)
- Band B: 2–3 (ages ~7–9)
- Band C: 4–5 (ages ~9–11)
- Band D: 6 (ages ~11–12)

Note: Pre‑K can use Band A with simpler targets and fewer options.

## Skill Progression Matrix

| Skill Domain | Band A (K–1) | Band B (2–3) | Band C (4–5) | Band D (6) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Phonemic Awareness | Identify initial sounds; rhyme matching | Blend 2–3 phonemes; segment simple words | Manipulate phonemes; onset/rime | Apply to unfamiliar words; analogy-based decoding |
| Decoding & Fluency | CVC words; short vowels | Digraphs, blends, multisyllable (2) | Syllable patterns, prefixes/suffixes | Morphology-based decoding, roots |
| Vocabulary | High‑frequency words; concrete nouns | Tier 1–2 words; simple synonyms | Tier 2 academic words; context clues | Nuanced word meaning; shades of meaning |
| Comprehension | Sequencing (first/next/last) | Cause/effect; main idea in short text | Summaries; inference; compare/contrast | Evaluate claims; synthesize across parts |
| Memory & Retrieval | Recall last scene; simple repetition | Spaced recall within episode | Recall across episodes; multi‑step | Strategic recall; note key details |
| Focus & Attention | Find target among 2–3 | Filter among 3–4 with distractors | Filter among 4–5; timed | Filter among 5–6; dual tasks |

## Template Parameter Ranges (By Band)

| Template | Band A | Band B | Band C | Band D |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Find & Point | 2–3 choices; bold targets | 3–4 choices; similar distractors | 4–5; subtle distractors | 5–6; multi‑step instruction |
| Match & Sort | 2–3 pairs; color cues | 3–4 pairs; fewer cues | 4–5 pairs; no cues | 5–6 pairs; timed or multi‑criteria |
| Sequence | 2–3 steps; short scenes | 3–4 steps; simple story | 4–5 steps; longer text | 5–6 steps; nested events |
| Recall | Immediate recall | 1–2 scene delay | Episode‑level recall | Multi‑episode recall |
| Focus & Filter | 2–3 items; high contrast | 3–4 items; similar items | 4–5 items; timed | 5–6 items; mixed modalities |

## Progression Rules
- Mastery threshold: 2 consecutive successes at current tier, then increase difficulty one notch.
- Support threshold: 2 consecutive struggles, then decrease difficulty one notch.
- Help usage: if “Help” used twice in a row, reduce complexity but keep target skill.
- No visible levels; difficulty changes are invisible to the child.

## Map Gating and Unlocks
- Regions unlock by completing a minimum number of episodes in a band.
- “Secret Moments” trigger on mastery streaks, not on failures.
- Portals are cosmetic but reinforce progress and curiosity.

## Assessment Checkpoints
- Every 5 episodes, a short review episode covers 2–3 templates only.
- Reports surface trends to parents, never to the child.

## Content Creation Guidelines
- Each episode uses 2–3 templates max.
- Variation comes from words, images, and audio, not new mechanics.
- Reuse backgrounds and props heavily within a region.

## Imagination Hooks (All Bands)
- “What if” prompts: imagine a new ending or new object in the scene.
- Open-ended reflection: “Tell TLVA what you noticed.”
- Creative choice moments: pick a path or color that changes the scene.
- Micro-story prompts: add one word to build a shared story.

## Imagination Mode (Drop-in Episode Template)
- Length: 60–120 seconds
- Placement: after a mini‑challenge or as a secret‑moment bonus
- Flow:
  - TLVA asks a “what if” question tied to the current skill
  - Child chooses from 2–3 playful options
  - Scene shifts visually to reflect the choice
  - TLVA mirrors the child’s choice back (“You made the forest glow blue!”)
- Goal: boost imagination and agency without new mechanics

## Delivery Notes
- Episode length target: 8–12 minutes for Band A–B, 10–14 minutes for Band C–D.
- Session length: 1–2 episodes max for younger learners.
