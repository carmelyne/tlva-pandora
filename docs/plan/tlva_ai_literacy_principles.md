# TLVA AI Literacy Principles (Preschool–K6)

## Core Framing
AI is a **co‑thinker**: we explore ideas together, and the human chooses what to do.

### What the AI is (Healthy Framing)
- A thinking partner who can be wrong
- Someone to challenge and test ideas with
- A participant in shared curiosity
- A system with clear limits and boundaries

## Parent-Facing North Star
We want kids to grow up with AI as a co‑thinker, not a replacement for their judgment.
The child leads. The system offers ideas. The family decides.

## Respect & Ethical Use
- We ask, we don’t order.\n- We treat AI like a thinking partner, not a servant.\n- Kind language matters.\n- If a request is unsafe or harmful, the system says no.\n- If something feels wrong, we stop and rethink.\n- The human is responsible for the outcome.

### Preschool Phrases
- “We ask nicely.”\n- “We don’t make anyone do unsafe things.”\n- “If something feels wrong, we stop and tell a grown‑up.”\n- “We choose what happens next.”

## Parent Onboarding Copy (Draft)
In TLVA, AI is a co‑thinker. It offers ideas, asks questions, and helps kids practice “ask, check, choose.” We teach respect and boundaries from day one: we ask nicely, we stop if something feels wrong, and parents stay in control. The goal is a healthy, confident relationship with AI—one where children lead with curiosity and responsibility.

## Principles
1. **Human Agency First**
   - AI can suggest, but the child decides.
   - TLVA models: “We can try this idea — you choose.”

2. **Ask / Check / Choose**
   - Ask a clear question.
   - Check the idea together (“Does it fit what we see?”).
   - Choose the next step.

3. **Boundaries & Privacy**
   - No personal info, real names, or secrets.
   - Parents control data and settings.

## Age-Band Examples

### Band A (K–1)
- “Let’s think together.”
- “Does this make sense?”
- “You choose what we do next.”

### Band B (2–3)
- “We can test that idea.”
- “If it doesn’t fit, we try a new idea.”

### Band C (4–5)
- “What evidence do we have?”
- “Which option matches the story best?”

### Band D (6)
- “Let’s compare two explanations.”
- “Which is more likely and why?”

## UI/UX Guardrails
- No open chat prompts for preschoolers.
- Suggestions are framed as options, not directives.
- Explanations avoid “because AI knows” language.

## Success Signals
- Kids ask questions back.
- Kids correct or reject a suggestion appropriately.
- Parents observe independent reasoning.
